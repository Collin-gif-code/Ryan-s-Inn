Since 2012

Our Story

From a private pub to an ever-growing collection, Ryan's Inn has been changing, expanding and collecting stories since 2012.

2012

Ryan's Inn is born

Ryan's Inn is established. What starts as a private pub becomes the beginning of a place built around good evenings, good company and, increasingly, a serious number of whiskey bottles.

2020

The wall comes down

During the coronavirus period, on Ryan's birthday, the wall is literally torn down and the pub is expanded. It marks the biggest transformation of Ryan's Inn so far. Since then, the pub has continued to evolve, with plenty of changes, additions and new bottles finding their way onto the shelves.

2026

Ryan's Inn goes online

The pub gets its own digital home. The whiskey collection, tasting notes, games, memories and history of Ryan's Inn can now live together in one place.
